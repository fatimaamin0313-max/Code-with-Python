# Lecture#14

  ## Sign Up And Create Profile On Fiverr:

* We cannot sign up our account using a public Wi-Fi or shared data.
* We can use only those accounts that we are allowed to use and cannot use them in any other way.
* Along with mobile data, if someone has already used their Fiverr account on the same device, take care while setting up your account so that the duplication problem does not occur.
* Make your Fiverr account completely original and genuine. Use your own details, such as your mobile number, profile picture, and email address, according to your NIC/CNIC.
* Use your profile to show your niche or skill. Mention the skill or course expertise you have.
