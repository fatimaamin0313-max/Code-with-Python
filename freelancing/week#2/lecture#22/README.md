## Gig Requirements

### What is Gig Requirment
- Information needed before starting work
- Colloected from client after order
- Defines project clarity and direction
- Essential for smooth workflow

### simple example good requirment
- What is the name of your brand or business?
- What does your business do?
- Which colors would you like to use in your logo?
- Do you have any sketches, mockup, or rough ideas to share?
- Do you have any inspiration or reference logos that you like?
- Do you have a preferred logo style, such as modren, minimalist, luxury, vintage or 3D?
