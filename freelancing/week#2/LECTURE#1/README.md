# LECTURE#1

## What is fiverr?

It is an international platform.
- Online freelancing marketpalce
- clients come to freelancers
- Gig-based service platform
- Global buyers and sellers
- Beginner friendly entry point

## What is a Gig?
- service listing on fiverr
- includes price, scope, delivery times
- defines what you offer
- Examples: "I will design a logo" 
- your main selling asset

## How freelancers get orders?

- Gig appare in fiverr search
- Attractive thumbnails increase clicks
- SEO (Key words) is important
- Cosistency improves visibility

## Fiverr earning model:

- No upfront cost to start
- Payment clears after order completion
- Fiverr takes 20% commission
- Earnings grow with reviews
- Scale with multiples Gigs

## Key success factors
- High quality gig presentation
- Fast response time
- Clear niche and positioning
- Consistent delivery quality
- client satisfaction and reviews
